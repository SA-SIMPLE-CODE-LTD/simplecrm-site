3. scrape or crawl the Service, or collect data from it, other than through our published APIs and within their rate limits;
4. overload the Service, or interfere with how it works, for example with excessive requests, bots or denial-of-service attacks;
5. get around usage limits, rate limits or plan limits, for example by creating several free accounts;
6. reverse engineer, decompile or copy the Service, or remove proprietary notices from it, except where the law allows it;
7. upload or send viruses, malware or any other harmful code;
8. use the Service for phishing, or to host or distribute malicious content;
9. impersonate any person or organization, or misrepresent who you are or who you work for;
10. use the Service to send spam or other unsolicited messages, including through automations, email notifications or forms, or to send messages to people who have opted out or whose contact details were bought or harvested;
11. use the Service for anything illegal, fraudulent or deceptive;
12. sell or share access to an account, or resell the Service without our written agreement;
13. use the Service to build or benchmark a competing product.
