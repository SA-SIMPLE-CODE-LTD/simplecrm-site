This Data Processing Agreement ("**DPA**") is part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) between {{legalName}} ("**Processor**") and the Customer ("**Controller**"). It applies when we process personal data on the Customer's behalf through {{name}}.

## 1. Subject matter and duration

We process personal data in Customer Data to provide the Service, for as long as the Customer uses it plus the deletion period in section 9.

## 2. Nature and purpose

Hosting, storage, retrieval, display, sharing within the Customer's workspace, automations the Customer configures, backups, support and security.

## 3. Types of data and data subjects

- **Data subjects:** the Customer's Users and anyone whose data the Customer stores in the Service (for example its own clients, leads or staff).
- **Types of data:** whatever the Customer chooses to store, typically names, contact details, job information, messages and files. The Customer should not store special categories of data unless it has a lawful basis to do so.

## 4. Our obligations

We will:

- process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions, which are these terms and the Customer's use of the Service;
- make sure people with access are bound by confidentiality;
- apply the security measures in section 6;
- help the Customer answer data subject requests and meet its security, breach notification and impact assessment obligations;
- notify the Customer without undue delay, and within [48] hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach;
- make available the information needed to show compliance, and allow reasonable audits [once a year, with 30 days' notice].

## 5. Subprocessors

The Customer authorizes the subprocessors on our [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) page. We will give [30] days' notice of a new subprocessor, during which the Customer may object. Each subprocessor is bound by data protection terms at least as protective as this DPA.

## 6. Security measures

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest.
- Role-based access control, column restrictions and private boards inside the Service.
- Argon2id password hashing, sign-in rate limiting and account lockout.
- Least-privilege production access with logging.
- Daily backups with point-in-time recovery.
- Dependency updates and security review for every change.

## 7. International transfers

Where personal data is transferred out of the EEA, UK or Israel to a country without an adequacy decision, the Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2, controller to processor) apply and are incorporated by reference. [Attach the SCCs and the UK Addendum.]

## 8. Data subject requests

If we receive a request from a data subject about Customer Data, we will pass it to the Customer and will not answer it ourselves unless the Customer instructs us to.

## 9. Deletion and return

When the Customer's account ends, the Customer may export its data for [30] days. After that we delete Customer Data, including from backups within [35] days, unless the law requires us to keep it.
